Important information

Website terms.

How to use this demonstration responsibly, and what this website does not provide.

This website is a preview. No lender registration information or actual loan rates have been verified. Nothing on this site represents an offer to lend.

1. Purpose of this website

The pages demonstrate the intended structure of a small personal-loan information campaign. They are not an operational application platform or a credit agreement. There is no active lender offer on this preview.

2. No lending decision or quotation

The planning tool is only a private way to organise the amount and purpose you have in mind. It does not calculate borrowing costs, assess affordability, check your credit record, approve an application or indicate that a particular loan amount is available.

3. No payment or personal-data requests

This website does not take payments or request identity documents, account details, income records or other sensitive financial information. Do not send such information to an unverified contact claiming to represent this website.

4. Accuracy and changes

General educational information may be updated. Actual offers, if introduced, will require accurate lender disclosure, terms and consumer documentation in accordance with applicable South African law. Do not rely on preview text to decide whether to enter a credit agreement.

5. Legal operator and contact details

The operating legal entity, business address, customer support contacts, complaints procedure and applicable NCR registration details remain to be verified. These must be inserted before launch.

6. External resources

External regulatory links are supplied for general reference. The National Credit Regulator is an independent regulator and does not endorse this website.

Preview and verification notice

Not a loan offer or application service. No loan applications are processed through this website.
