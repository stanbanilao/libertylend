Information and privacy

Privacy notice.

What this preview website does with information, and what must be confirmed before a real lending enquiry service can launch.

Preview only: Liberty Lend SA has not yet provided a verified legal-entity name, information officer contact, NCR registration or live processing details. This notice is a transparent description of the current static preview, not a final operational POPIA policy.

1. Information you enter

The optional planning tool accepts only a purpose selection and an amount. This information is processed in your browser to display a planning note. It is not sent to a Liberty Lend SA server, saved in browser storage or used for credit assessments. Do not enter your identity number, banking details or other sensitive information.

2. Technical information

Web hosting providers may process standard connection details, including IP addresses and request logs, to deliver the website and maintain its security. The hosting provider and its retention policy must be identified before a public launch. This version does not include tracking pixels, third-party analytics, online application forms or external fonts.

3. Cookies and storage

The supplied website code does not set advertising cookies or persist the planning inputs in local storage. If third-party hosting introduces cookies, the deployed service must update this notice to explain them.

4. Future enquiries

Online applications are disabled. Before enabling enquiries, the responsible party must publish its legal identity, contact details, purposes for collecting information, lawful processing basis, recipients and processors, safeguards, retention period, and the process for exercising privacy rights. A separate secure application service should handle any identity, employment, bank or affordability information.

5. Your rights

South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) may give data subjects rights to request access to, correction of or deletion of personal information, depending on the circumstances. Contact details for the responsible party and Information Officer must be added before launch. For general regulatory information visit the Information Regulator website.

6. Updates

This notice describes the preview code as delivered in November 2026. It must be reviewed and dated when the live service's operator, data flows and hosting arrangements are confirmed.

Preview and verification notice

Not a loan offer or application service. No loan applications are processed through this website.
