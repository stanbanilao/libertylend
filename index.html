Good questions

Straight answers.

Understand what this site can and cannot do before taking the next step.

Can I apply for a loan here today?

Not yet. This is a website preview, and online loan enquiries are not currently being accepted. Applications can only be enabled after the credit provider's details, lending terms and privacy processes have been verified.

Is approval guaranteed?

No. A legitimate lending decision is subject to the credit provider's required assessment of your circumstances and affordability. This site cannot approve credit.

How much will a loan cost?

Actual interest rates, initiation fees, service fees and any compulsory insurance have not been provided or verified for this website. Do not assume a repayment amount until you receive a full quotation from the lender.

Does this planning tool check my credit record?

No. The planning tool runs only in your browser. It does not request identity or banking details, send your inputs to a server, or initiate a credit enquiry.

What should I consider before borrowing?

Check your regular expenses, existing commitments and ability to make each repayment. Compare the total cost, not only the monthly instalment, and never borrow more than you can reasonably afford.
